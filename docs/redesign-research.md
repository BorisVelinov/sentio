# Redesign research

Sites reviewed in the browser on 2026-11-05. We borrow techniques only, never layouts, assets or code.

| Site | Status | What it does well | What Sentio borrows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Arc'teryx System 0](https://arcteryx.com/us/en/explore/system0/) | Awwwards nominee | One serif headline stays pinned while full-bleed macro material changes behind it (water on rock, then a zipper). Material carries the story. | Pinned type over changing material. This is the closest model for snow and dirt as materials. |
| [Spyker Cars](https://spykercars.com/) | Awwwards Site of the Day, 30 Oct 2026 | Condensed capitals, white space, editorial offset images, macro craft detail with small captions. | Offset macro detail shots with short captions. Light editorial sections to break up dark ones. |
| [Forge Automotive](https://forgeautomotive.co.uk/) | Awwwards Site of the Day, 25 Oct 2026 | Black opening with a statement that types itself out, behind a thin progress line. Only the opening was seen: the page was still loading. | A short HUD-style boot line, kept brief so it never delays the product. |
| [Apple Vision Pro](https://www.apple.com/apple-vision-pro/) | Reference | Large centred statement screens with nothing else on them. Product on pale grey. Sticky sub-nav with a buy button. | Statement screens, a product sub-nav, and the discipline of one idea per screen. |
| [Teenage Engineering OP-1](https://teenage.engineering/products/op-1) | Reference | Huge product on flat warm grey, small lowercase labels, a personality that is unmistakably its own. | Treat the product like an object in a catalogue. Tiny technical labels as brand voice. |
| [Polestar 3](https://www.polestar.com/us/polestar-3/) | Main reference for everything below the hero | Zero rounded corners, one font at modest sizes, full-bleed images, staggered image pairs, thin rules, a sticky section nav with square buttons, plain spec tables. | The whole below-the-hero system: statements on the left, staggered real photos, hairlines instead of boxes. |
| [Rivian R1T](https://rivian.com/r1t) | Reference | Rugged product with a calm, premium interface. Dirt and outdoors shown without any aggression. Consent popup covered part of the view. | Proof that dirt can feel premium: warm neutrals, large image cards with short titles. |

Not reviewed: Nothing (the page tried returned a 404), The FWA and CSS Design Awards.

## Takeaways

1. The best work here lets material and image do the talking, with very little interface around them.
2. The strongest pages hold type still and change the world behind it. That fits "two terrains, one lens" directly.
3. Small technical labels next to huge type read as expensive. The HUD language gives Sentio its own version of this.
4. Long preloaders are a weakness on several of these sites. Sentio should show the product immediately.
